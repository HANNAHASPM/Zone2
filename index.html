    Setting your band

    You don't need an FTP test. Ride your usual hour at the effort you already treat as zone 2 and watch where the 30-second average power settles once you're warm, roughly ten minutes in. That number is your zone 2 power. Set the band about 15 W either side and steer by power from then on.

    The gauge judges the 30-second average, not the instant figure. Flywheel power estimates jump with every pedal stroke, and chasing the raw number would have you surging all hour.

    Speed and distance come from converting your watts into a real road speed, not from the bike's own speed reading — the bike turns pedalling rate straight into km/h, which flatters light fast spinning. Same calculation as the family tour page, so the two agree. 100 W is about 26 km/h; 140 W about 29 km/h.
