Setting your band

You don't need an FTP test to start. Ride your usual hour at the heart rate you already treat as zone 2, and watch what the 30-second average power settles at once your HR is steady — roughly 10 minutes in, since HR lags. That number is your zone 2 power. Set the band about 15 W either side of it and steer by power from then on, using HR only as a sanity check.

The 30-second average is what the gauge judges, not the instant figure. Flywheel power estimates jump around with every pedal stroke, and chasing the raw number would have you surging all hour.

Pw:Hr drift compares power-per-heartbeat in the first half of the ride against the second half. Under about 5% means you held a true aerobic effort. Much more than that means the pace was too hard to be zone 2, however it felt at the time. It needs both a bike and an HR connection, and at least 20 minutes.
