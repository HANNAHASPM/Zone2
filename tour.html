      How distance is worked out

      The bike's own speed reading turns pedalling rate straight into km/h, which flatters light fast spinning — it once credited a 40-watt rider with 33 km/h. So this page ignores it and works out road speed from watts instead, using the standard cycling power model. Everyone is scored as the same standard rider, so a hard effort always travels further than an easy one no matter who is on the bike. 40 W makes about 17 km/h, 100 W about 26 km/h, 200 W about 34 km/h.
